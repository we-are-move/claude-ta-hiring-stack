# Tone of Voice Template — company voice for the Talent Persuasion Framework

## How to fill this in

Be specific. Vague answers give vague output. If asked about your target candidate, do not write "senior engineers." Write "senior product engineers at Series B to D scale-ups who own services end-to-end and are frustrated their current company is chasing growth over engineering quality." The more specific you are, the more the framework can match your voice.

Answer in full sentences where possible. The framework reads these answers as raw material for understanding how the company speaks.

If you do not know an answer yet, write "we are still working this out" rather than guessing. The framework will work better as your answers sharpen over time. Treat this as a living document. Update it whenever your employer brand evolves.

When you have filled it in (or updated it), save the file where Claude can see it — your project folder or the folder you've connected to Claude. The framework will reference it on every outreach generation.
